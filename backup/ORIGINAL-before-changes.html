How it works

From your first move to your next rating class.

Every student enters the same way, regardless of where they're starting from.

01

Evaluation game

Play a single rated game against a resident coach so we can see how you actually think, not just what you know.

02

Placement

Your game is reviewed against our three-stage curriculum and you're placed at the point that matches your calculation, not your rating alone.

03

Coach match

You're paired with a coach whose playing style and teaching pace fit how you learn — not assigned at random.

04

Weekly cadence

A standing weekly session, structured drills between sessions, and a review of every rated game you play.
